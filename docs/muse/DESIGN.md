# Muse design

The Muse edition should feel calm, personal, and quietly capable, and look like a current Apple Mac app (owner-approved redesign): a rounded content window, floating glass sidebar and inspector, iOS inset grouped lists, colored app tiles, the system font, blue only for the primary action, and Nova shown as a live shader orb. It keeps the richness of the Aiden concept's cards (a deck preview, "finished while you were away", "one yes before I send this"). Light-first, with a matching dark theme. Muse is Aiden's only edition now (the old multi-bot mode has been retired); the styling here is still scoped under `[data-product="muse"]`, which the app sets unconditionally.

## The Muse

The Muse is **Aiden**, drawn as a single round, friendly face (`BotAvatar` with `face="muse"`, `packages/ui-web/src/bot-avatar.tsx`). The body is a soft rounded blob filled with the bot's identity color (sky by default) and shaded with a subtle depth gradient (lighter top-left, slightly deeper bottom-right, built only from the illustration's own ink/shine constants layered over that color — never a new hex), plus a soft rim light along the top edge and a gentle contact shadow underneath. Two dark oval eyes with glints, blush cheeks, and a small gold spark on top complete the silhouette; it reads at 24–32px and is delightful at 96–160px (welcome 160px, onboarding 120px, auth 88px).

His face changes with `data-muse-state` (`idle | thinking | working | waiting`, derived by `museAvatarState`, never a free string):
- **idle** — relaxed smile, a slow blink, the spark floating.
- **thinking** — eyes glance up-right, one eyebrow raised, a small "hmm" mouth, three thought dots pulsing.
- **working** — eyes slightly narrowed, a small determined smile, a gentle sway, the spark spinning.
- **waiting** — both eyebrows up, an open happy mouth, a small waving hand at one side, a hop, and the existing Ask-count badge.

Expressions are structural (conditional SVG keyed on state, so they still show under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the breathing, blinking, swaying, hopping, spinning and waving are CSS animations on top, all turned off under reduced motion. He is our own character, not a bank's logo.

**The orb.** In the Muse UI, Nova is a live shader orb (`NovaOrb`, `apps/web/src/components/ai/orb/`), not a face: the sidebar's identity (34px), the inspector header (26px), the start page (96px), the toolbar below `xl` (22px) and every working row (22px). Shader inspired by "Shader Reminder" by Daniela Muntyan (CC BY 4.0); original implementation. Props: `size` and `state` (`idle | working`); without a `state` it follows the shell's live presence (`NovaPresenceProvider`, fed by `useNovaWork`: a running turn or any in-progress Activity), so every orb brightens and speeds up together while Nova works. One shared renderer (`renderer.ts`) owns a single hidden WebGL context: each frame it draws every visible orb at that orb's own pixel size and copies it into the orb's 2D canvas, so a page never holds more than one context however many orbs it shows. One `requestAnimationFrame` loop drives all orbs; it stops while the tab is hidden and while no orb is on screen (IntersectionObserver); DPR is capped at 2; small orbs (< 40px) keep an energy floor so they never look switched off. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` each orb draws one still frame (redrawn on a state or theme change). Without WebGL, or after a context loss, the orb is a still radial gradient of the same size (`.nova-orb`), so nothing shifts. The GL context is released when the last orb unmounts.

**Bloop.** Bloop stays hidden in the Muse UI; the orb replaces it. Wherever the Muse's face appears, `BotAvatar face="muse"` draws it as a 3D jelly drop, `BloopAvatar` in `apps/web/src/components/ai/bloop/`, supplied app-wide through `LiveMuseFaceProvider` so no surface changes its own call. The four states and the Ask badge are the same as the static face. The body is a soft, nearly opaque satin gumdrop (never glass, no hard highlights) in the Muse's identity color with a gentle lighter-top gradient derived from it, over a calm contact shadow; the eyes, brows and cheeks reuse the face constants above, and the same gold spark floats over its head, twinkling and spinning while the Muse works. The thought cloud and "…" bubble are drawn from the `--card`, `--border` and `--foreground` tokens, so both themes work. Props appear from 32px up; smaller faces are framed tighter without them. One shared WebGL renderer (`live.ts`) draws every face onto its own 2D canvas, so a page never holds more than one WebGL context; a face claims one of 12 live slots when it scrolls into view and the rest stay static. The static SVG face renders first and stays when WebGL is unavailable; three.js loads lazily, like the aurora. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` each face draws a still frame.

## Foundations

**Color.** Semantic tokens only (`@aiden/ui-tokens`: `museLightTokens`, `museDarkTokens`); never a hex in a component. Surfaces: `window` (the ground), `content` (the rounded window), `glass` + `glass-line` (floating panels), `group` (grouped lists and cards), `separator` (inset hairlines), `selection` / `selection-strong` (fills); text `ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`. Owner-approved exceptions to the monochrome rule, for the Mac-app look: `tint` (the accent, `#0071e3` / `#0a84ff`: the primary action, the selected sidebar row, the person's bubble, the send button), `tint-ink`, `link`, `ok`, `warn`, `alert` (the red count badge), the app `tile-<color>-from/to` gradients (blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, red, indigo, teal, gray), one signature color per section (`sig-goals` green, `sig-feed` pink, `sig-ideas` orange, `sig-library` indigo, `sig-waiting` orange, `sig-forks` purple), the dark result tile's `media-*`, and `fork-1..6`. shadcn's `primary` stays ink. Keyboard focus is the accent at 50% (`ring`).

**Type.** The system font first (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text"`), Instrument Sans as the fallback where there is no San Francisco, inherited from the root and never set per component. Chrome sizes: sidebar rows 13px, section headers 11px/700 `ink-3`, the toolbar title 15px/600, the conversation 15px/1.47, inspector rows 14px with 12px subtitles. The screen scale (`MUSE_TYPE` in `apps/web/src/pages/muse/ui`):
- `chromeTitle` (15.5px semibold): the screen's name in the shared top bar.
- `pageTitle` (28px semibold): a screen that reads like its own page (Ideas' hero, a Goal's own title).
- `sectionTitle` (15px semibold): a group heading inside a screen ("Plan", "Paused", "Productivity").
- `cardTitle` (16px semibold): a card's or row's own title (a Goal, a Post, a Library item, an Idea).
- `body` (15px, relaxed leading): reading text inside a card or row.
- `meta` (13px, muted): a quiet fact line — dates, counts, sources.
- `label` (`font-mono`, 11px, uppercase, `tracking-[0.08em]`): a small tag, sparingly — never a whole heading.

Instrument Serif (`font-display`) is reserved for the signed-out welcome and auth screens; it never appears on an in-app section header or empty state.

**Space and shape.** Content in a centered 720px column (`MuseColumn`) or, for screens that fill the panel (Goals, Library), a wide left-aligned one (`MuseWideColumn`); 32px side padding on desktop, 20px on phones. Cards: 16px radius, 1px border, 16–20px padding, no shadow at rest. Elevation (`shadow-float`) only for things that float: the composer, popovers, sheets, hovered interactive cards. Gaps between cards 12px; between sections 40px.

**Motion.** 150ms color/border transitions; nothing bounces except the Muse face. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Window.** The signed-in shell is a Mac window: the `window` ground with 8px around one `content` window, 22px rounded with a soft shadow (`.nova-window`). Inside it the sidebar (244px, 54px collapsed) and the inspector (300px) float as glass panels inset 8px (`.nova-glass`: the translucent `glass` fill, a 30px saturating backdrop blur, a half-pixel `glass-line`, 18px radius); the computer, settings and artifact side panels use the same glass. The Conversation and the other screens sit flat on the content window. On phones the window is full-bleed. The toolbar is 56px with no hairline: the title, a quiet subtitle (the live status, or "New" on the start page) and the header actions in one glass pill group. Grouped lists are `.nova-group` (16px radius, `group` fill, inset `separator` lines starting 52px in); colored tiles are `NovaTile` (`.nova-tile`, 30% radius, a top-to-bottom tile gradient, a white glyph).

## Building blocks

Import from `apps/web/src/pages/muse/ui`: `MuseScreen` (takes an optional `header`), `ScreenHeader` (the one top chrome bar — same height, border and title style everywhere, shared by the Conversation and every other section), `MuseColumn`, `MuseWideColumn`, `Section`, `Eyebrow`, `Surface` (`default | attention | quiet`, `interactive`), `StatusPill` (`neutral | live | attention | done`), `Chip`, `SuggestionChips` (quiet chips, each with an optional small icon, that start a Conversation), `DetailRows`, `Progress`, `EmptyState` (a headline, one line, and optional suggestion chips; the Muse's face only when asked). Use them before writing new chrome. Buttons, inputs, tabs, sheets, dialogs come from `@aiden/ui-web`. Icons: `lucide-react`, 16px, `strokeWidth={1.75}`.

## Card patterns (from the Aiden concept)

- **Waiting on you (Ask).** `Surface tone="attention"`. Header row: small icon + a plain-language title in the Muse's voice ("One yes before I send this", "Which evenings work?"). For approvals, show what will happen with `DetailRows` (To, Subject, Attached, Body…) parsed from the Ask's `detail` when it has `Key: value` lines, otherwise the detail as quiet text. Actions: the primary choice as a solid ink button ("Send it"), the rest as outline/ghost ("Let me edit first", "Not now"). Source (Goal title or "Conversation") as a mono eyebrow.
- **Finished while you were away (Goal report Post).** `Surface` with a 2px `warning` left edge, mono eyebrow "Finished while you were away · Tue" (relative day), the report in the Muse's voice, and a quiet link to the Goal.
- **Found for you (Followed-topic Post).** `Surface`, eyebrow with the topic and source host, title, two-line summary, "Read" link opening the source.
- **Proposal.** `Surface tone="attention"`: reason in one sentence, the proposed plan as a numbered list with added items marked and removed items struck through, "Accept plan" (solid) and "Keep current" (ghost).
- **Ideas.** Under a "Things I could start now" eyebrow: `Chip`s, grouped by area only when there are more than six.
- **Working line** (`chrome/ActivityLine.tsx`). One piece of work in two shapes. In the inspector (`list`): a 52px grouped-list row, a small live orb while it works or a colored tile for its source (turn blue, side chat teal, background indigo, housekeeping yellow, scheduled orange, goal green; failed red, cancelled gray), the title over "Now · <live step>" or the outcome, a mono figure (elapsed, else the clock) and a chevron; a Helper's parts are indented rows of the same group. In the conversation (`inline`, and `WorkingRow` for a reply in progress): a soft rounded row on `window` with a tiny orb, the title, the step and a timer; a Helper's parts hang beneath on a thin `line` connector.
- **Waiting on you decision** (`asks/AskDecisionCard.tsx`, mapped by `askDecision`). In the inspector: a grouped card with a tile for the Ask's kind, the title and a quiet line (the same title rules as `AskItem`), then two equal buttons: the second choice as the secondary on the left, the first choice in the accent on the right, answered with the choice ids. An Ask that needs typing or has more than two choices offers one "Answer" button that opens Waiting on you.
- **Failed replies.** A failed reply is a quiet inline row on Nova's side: a small alert mark and the note in `ink-3`. Several in a row fold into one line ("3 failed attempts") with a small Show / Hide link listing each note and its time.
- **Results** (Conversation, `components/MediaTile.tsx`). Every file Nova delivers is a media tile, like an Apple TV card: a dark rounded card (28px) with a deep blue glow (`media-*`), a brand line ("Nova Report" for a document, else the file kind) with a small blue tile, a big title, art on the right, and a white "Open" pill with "Kind • size" at the bottom. The art is the real preview when Nova has one (a small image or page thumbnail, or a saved file's inline preview, framed like a window rising from the bottom edge); otherwise it is drawn for the kind (bars for a report, stacked slides for a deck, a browser window for a page, a landscape for an image, sheets for a file). Download (and Open in new tab, in a menu for saved files) stay at the end of the bottom row; versions sit beside the brand.
- **Status.** Nothing while idle. While thinking/working: a small animated face plus a shimmering verb next to the Muse's name or under it ("Thinking…", "Browsing…", "Running code…", "Writing a file…") — no border, no counters, no timestamps. While an Ask is open: a clickable `StatusPill` ("Needs you", `attention` tone) that opens Waiting. See `apps/web/src/pages/muse/chrome/useMuseLiveState.ts` and `MuseLiveStatus.tsx`.

## Screens

Every section shares one `ScreenHeader` chrome bar at the top (same height, padding, border and title style as the Conversation's, with a right-side actions slot) and the same column widths and top spacing below it.

- **Sidebar.** A glass panel (see Window). At the top, Nova: the live orb, the name, and a status line with a green dot ("Ready", or "Working on N things" from the live runs and Activity feed), with the sidebar toggle on the right. Then the Conversation row, its side chats under a "Side chats" header and its forks by state ("Working · 1", "Open · 2", "Added to Conversation"), as 28px rows with small 13px glyphs (forks in their own color with a reply-count badge in that color); then a "Nova" header over Goals, Feed, Ideas, Library and Waiting on you. Rows are 32px, 13px, with Nova's own filled glyphs (`chrome/NovaGlyphs.tsx`, drawn for Nova, not Apple's SF Symbols) in each section's signature color; the selected row is solid `tint` with white text and glyph; hover is `selection`. Headers are 11px/700 `ink-3`. Counts are badges: red `alert` for Waiting on you, quiet for Goals. Under the places, up to three active Goals with a tiny progress ring. The footer is the person (a gray avatar and name) with a Settings gear; collapsed, the sidebar is a 54px icon rail with tooltips.
- **Conversation.** The toolbar (see Window). Messages in the 720px column at 15px/1.47; the person's messages in `tint` bubbles with white text (18px radius) on the right, Nova's replies without a bubble, their markdown on one rhythm (code on the `bubble` surface in Geist Mono 13px). The Ask field is a rounded 24px field on `group` with a hairline and a soft drop (`.nova-ask`, an accent halo on focus), the send button a round `tint` button once there is something to send, attach and voice in `ink-3`; it reads "Ask Nova". First run: Nova's orb at 96px, the time-of-day greeting (30px/600) and one muted line, the Ask field right under it, then a row of app tiles (48px colored tiles with 12px labels: Plan week, New goal, Research, Explore) that send their starting prompts. The first message glides the composer down to its dock (off under reduced motion).
- **Inspector (context panel).** A glass panel beside the Conversation: the orb, Nova's name and a small "Connected" pill, a segmented control (Activity, Memory, Context), then the tab. Activity puts what waits on the person first as decision cards, then live work ("Working now") and settled work grouped by day, each group an inset grouped list under a 13px semibold header with a quiet count; nothing yet is one muted line, and a feed that has not answered within a few seconds (or a hidden tab) shows that line rather than a skeleton. Context lists Waiting on you, In progress and Coming up as grouped lists.
- **Forks** (ADR 0010, `apps/web/src/pages/muse/forks/`). A message's hover toolbar floats above it with Fork; Fork opens "lift and ask" (the chat blurs, the message lifts onto a solid card, one question box under it). Under a message, one fork is an iMessage reply curve with its title and replies, several are one pill (up to three pips, "+N", "N forks · M open") that fans out into a list; an added fork's summary is a green-check "From a fork:" line. A fork opens over the blurred Conversation inside the chat panel: anchor lifted, sibling forks as pills, replies hanging off one line, the person's bubbles and the composer in the fork's color, then "Add to Conversation", "Open as side chat", "Archive". Open and working forks take `fork-1..6` by a hash of their id; added and archived ones are `ink-3`. A gutter on the Conversation's end edge marks messages with forks; the sidebar lists Working and Open forks under the Conversation with Added and Archived folded into counts; "Chat | Forks" in the header switches to the All forks list. Fixture: `/dev/forks`.
- **Section screens.** Goals, Feed, Ideas and Library share the 56px toolbar (`ScreenHeader`) and an 860px column (`MuseWideCenter`) that opens with a hero (`ScreenHero`): the section's 44px colored tile, a 28px bold title over one quiet line, and an optional control on the right.
- **Goals.** Hero (green tile) with "N active · N need you" and a "New goal" accent button that sends the existing "Start a new goal" prompt. A Goal with an open plan shows a decision card at the top ("A plan is ready for “…”" with its first step; Not now / Start this plan, or Keep current / Use the new plan), using the same accept and dismiss calls as the detail view. Goals are cards in a grid (`nova-card`, min 240px): a progress ring (green, blue, purple in turn; orange while waiting on you; gray when paused) with "3 of 5", the title, "Next: …", and a quiet status and due line. Detail keeps its layout: the ring in the Goals color, the proposal card with the equal button pair.
- **Feed.** Hero (red tile). Followed topics are pills, each with its own × to stop following, and "+ Follow a topic" opening a field with one-tap suggestions (open from the start while nothing is followed). Open Asks as decision cards. Posts are media tiles in a 1.4fr / 1fr grid: a dark card with a glow (topic findings cycle blue, warm and pink; a Goal report is green), an uppercase kicker (the topic or "From your goal", and when), the title, a two-line preview, a white "Read" pill that opens the full text, and the source hosts. Empty: the example morning on the window fill.
- **Ideas.** Hero (orange tile). An inset grouped list (by area under a 13px header when that helps): the area's colored tile with the Idea's icon, the Idea and its detail, then Dismiss and an accent Do it.
- **Library.** The toolbar holds the search field and a "Made for you | Skills" segmented control; the hero (indigo tile) holds the kind filter as a segmented control with counts. A preview grid (min 170px): a 4:3 preview on the window fill with a hairline (the real thumbnail for small images and pages; otherwise drawn art: a report as a small dark result tile, a deck as a blue slide, a page or file as a sheet of paper, an image as a landscape), then the name and "Kind · when"; Open, Download and the menu float on the preview on hover.
- **Waiting on you.** A right sheet on the content fill: the orange tile, the title and "I ask before doing anything I can't undo.", then each open Ask as a big decision card (`AskItem`: a 44px tile for its kind, the title, where it came from, what it would do, then two equal buttons, the secondary on the left and the accent primary on the right; more choices stack; a typed answer gets a field and Send). The Feed and the Conversation's approval cards use the same card. Below, a "Decided" grouped list of what was answered from the sheet this session, with the choice made.
- **Settings.** A rounded window with a glass sidebar: each section as a 22px colored tile (Nova as its orb), the selected one solid accent. Sections are iOS inset grouped lists (`SettingsGroup`, 13px semibold headers): 52px rows with a 28px tile, the label, a value in `ink-3` and a chevron; toggles are green iOS switches; separators start after the tile, or at the text for rows without one.
- **Memory tab.** Each section is a grouped list under a 13px header with its count and an "Edit" / "Done" link that keeps the rows' actions (source, edit, forget) in view; rows carry a tile (you blue, projects green, open questions purple, how Nova works with you indigo, people an orange tile with their initials), commitments a checkbox. Days are a grouped list too.
- **Onboarding.** One centered 440px column, serif question as the title ("What should I call you?"), one input, one ink button; the Muse face large on the color step.

## Copy

The Muse speaks in the first person and plainly ("I have not booked anything.", "One yes before I send this"). Labels are short. No explainer paragraphs; empty states are one line.
